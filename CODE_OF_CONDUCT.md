# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

Contributors and maintainers should make participation respectful and useful. Harassment, discrimination, personal attacks, intimidation, and publishing another person's private information are not acceptable.

Give feedback about ideas and code, describe impact plainly, and make room for different experience levels and backgrounds. Maintainers may remove content or restrict participation when needed to protect the community.

## Reporting

Report conduct concerns privately through [GitHub's contact support](https://support.github.com/contact) and identify the CriteriaTrace repository. Do not use public issues for sensitive reports. Project maintainers will review reports promptly and keep them private to the extent practical.

This policy is adapted in spirit from the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).
